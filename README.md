# CYBERVAULT 3D // Next-Level Smart Expense Tracker & Spatial Financial Core 💎🌐

An ultra-modern, cyberpunk-inspired **3D Smart Expense Tracker** built with **Three.js**, **WebGL**, **Web Audio API**, and **AI-Driven Financial Intelligence**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🪐 Real-Time 3D Spatial Financial Viewport (Three.js)
- **Holographic Core (Wealth Orb)**: The core crystal and kinetic cyber cage rotate smoothly, changing their energy color, pulse velocity, and aura based on your **Financial Health Score (0-100%)**.
- **Dynamic 3D Category Pillars**: Each spending category has a 3D hexagonal tower in an orbital ring. Their heights dynamically animate with **GSAP** proportional to your spending!
- **Interactive 3D Raycasting**: Hover over any 3D pillar to view a dynamic spatial tooltip displaying total spend, percentage, and transaction count. **Click any pillar to filter the ledger** to that category!
- **Cinematic Camera Modes**: Toggle between **Holographic Core**, **3D Expense Towers**, and **Orbital Rings** views, plus free orbit controls (rotate, zoom, pan).
- **Nebula Particle Field**: Over 1,000 floating data particles reacting to ambient lighting.

### 2. 🧠 AI Natural Language Smart Prompt
- Type transactions naturally without manual dropdowns:
  - *"Spent 850 on grocery"*
  - *"Paid 1200 for petrol"*
  - *"Salary 75000"*
  - *"Coffee 240"*
  - *"Freelance payment 25k"*
- Automatically detects the **amount**, determines **income vs. expense**, classifies the **category**, and logs it instantly.

### 3. 🛡️ Autonomous Financial Copilot & Telemetry
- **Financial Health Score (0-100%)**: Evaluates savings retention and monthly budget burn rate.
- **Runway Assessment**: Estimates your liquid operational runway in months.
- **Concentration Risk Alerts**: Warns when a single category absorbs a dangerous portion of your income.
- **Monthly Target Vault**: Live progress bar with Safe (<70%), Warning (70-90%), and Danger (>90%) levels.

### 4. 🔊 Synthesized Web Audio Sound FX
- Uses the native browser **Web Audio API** to generate futuristic sci-fi sound effects (click, coin drop, melodic income chime, and budget warning alert). No external audio files needed!

### 5. 📊 Ledger & Data Management
- Multi-Currency Support: **₹ INR**, **$ USD**, **€ EUR**, **£ GBP**, **¥ JPY**.
- Search, filter by Type (All / Expense / Income), and isolate by 3D Category.
- **CSV Export** for spreadsheets & **JSON Backup/Restore**.
- Persisted locally in `localStorage` with pre-configured demo transactions.
